Project Abstract
The Inquiry and Calling Management System is a specialized web-based ERP module developed for Gyanmanjari Innovative The Inquiry and Calling Management System is a specialized web-based ERP module developed for Gyanmanjari Innovative University (GMIU) to digitize the student recruitment process. The platform centralizes prospective student inquiries, enabling efficient registration and assignment of leads to counselors.

Key features include:

Lead Management: Facilitates candidate registration, CSV data ingestion, and systematic assignment.
Tracking & Reporting: Enables real-time recording of calling conversations, automated daily task reports, and marketing visit tracking.
Secure Access: Implements a multi-tiered permission system for stakeholders like IT, Reception, and Counselors.
Performance Monitoring: Provides dashboards to analyze staff productivity and lead conversion rates.
This system replaces manual tracking with a robust, data-driven approach, significantly enhancing admission department efficiency and ensuring no prospective student inquiry goes unattended.
